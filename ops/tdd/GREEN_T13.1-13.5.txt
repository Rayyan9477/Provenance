================ 6. What the synthesis found that reading would not ============

Two defects were caught by Template.from_stack and by nothing else. Both would
have synthesised cleanly under `cdk synth` and then failed at deploy time.

  a) PvMessagingStack held an intra-stack RESOURCE cycle:
       WorkerDlqPolicy -> AdvocateRule -> AdvocateQueue -> AdvocateDlq
         -> AdvocateQueue
     The DLQ's redriveAllowPolicy named the source queue by GetAtt while the
     queue's RedrivePolicy already named the DLQ. `python app.py` emitted the
     template happily; Template.from_stack refused it with "Template is
     undeployable". Fixed by building the source-queue ARN from the canonical
     queue name, which is a contract value.

  b) Two cross-STACK cycles, both from CDK writing a resource policy into the
     stack that owns the resource rather than the stack that made the grant:
       - EventBridge rule DLQs (rule in Compute, queue in Messaging)
       - Secret.grantRead on a CMK-encrypted secret, which grants kms:Decrypt
         through a ViaServicePrincipal and can only be expressed in the KEY's
         resource policy (key in Foundation, readers in Compute and Api)
     Both resolved and documented in the source as D-13-003 and D-13-010.
